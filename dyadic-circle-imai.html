Dyadic Circle (Imai)

Signed dyadic circular embedding after Y. Imai, Geometric Extensions of Dyadic Circular Embeddings to the Integers (2026, HAL hal-05858260v1, CC BY 4.0). For n > 0, θ(n) = π(n − 2k)/2k with k = ⌊log₂ n⌋, and θ(−n) = θ(n) + π, so each negative integer sits antipodal to its positive counterpart. The paper's map is Tm(n) = n/2 (n even), m·n (n odd), with no additive term; set c = 1 for the Collatz-type map 3n+1. Definitions and caveats: docs/imai-embedding-notes.md.
