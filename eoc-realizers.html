EOC Three-Class Realizer Comparison

Accelerated map T(m) = (3m+1)/2a(m) on odd m. Each valuation word (a1,…,aN) is realized by one residue class mod 2SN+1; synthetic words are drawn from their least positive realizer r(D). Notation follows De Jesús, A Global Occupation Conjecture for the Accelerated 3x+1 Map, Rev. 7 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22906673): drift Rn = Sn − n·log₂3, occupation Oc = #{n : Rn ≤ c}, endpoint depth E(D) = SN − log₂ r(D).

    Carry-walk residue tower

    Modulo q = pe the carry sum factors through the walk u0 = 1, uj+1 = uj·2dj·3−1 on Ke = ⟨2,3⟩ ≤ (ℤ/qℤ)×, since CN ≡ 3N−1·Σ uj. Following De Jesús, Exact Spectral Inheritance in Prime-Power Resolution Towers (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23088511), each walk's occupation density fe on Ke is split along He = J(He−1) ⊕ Ze: the part inherited from level e−1 and the genuinely new part. The new-sector energy ‖fe − J fe−1‖² is plotted relative to its i.i.d.-uniform expectation dim Ze / N, so 1 is sampling noise, values far below 1 mean a low-discrepancy walk, and values far above 1 mean new fine structure. This uses the paper's state space and splitting only. The transfer operators and the gap g0(p, e) are not computed here.

    What the box is detecting

    By the Eliahou–Rozier identity (EOC Lem. 2.2), every point of a trajectory satisfies log₂ mᵢ = log₂ m₀ − Rᵢ + Eᵢ, where the carry excess Eᵢ ≥ 0 is tiny once m is large. So each path is the drift walk Ri of its word, shifted by the size of its seed. The visible differences reduce to two numbers:

    1. Endpoint depth E(D) = SN − log₂ r(D), which is where the realizer sits inside its residue class mod 2SN+1. For every synthetic class (periodic, Sturmian, random control) the least realizer is an ordinary member of its class, so E(D) = O(1) and the path sits at the ceiling of the box. Actual extremal orbits have E(D)/SN ≈ 0.9: they come from seeds about 20.9·S below the top of the class. This is the residue axis of EOC. The realizer frontier rmin(N, c) asks how deep E(D) can go across all c-confined words, and record chronology (Prop. 6.4) says the L₁ record holders are exactly that frontier.
    2. Shape of the drift walk. Periodic and Sturmian words are balanced (|Si − iμ| < 1); the critical ones stay inside a unit band of R, which is band rigidity (EOC App. A), and so are confined for their whole length. Actual orbits and random words wander about √N.

    Periodic vs Sturmian. On every picture-level statistic the two look the same: balanced, made of letters in {1,2}, never visiting 5 mod 8, with E(D) = O(1). The box cannot separate them. What separates them is height amortization ρN = log₂H / SN (EOC Obs. 5.9). The convergent periodic words have ρN well below 1, while every Sturmian word has ρN > 1. Arithmetically the two are linked by the 2-adic depth law in the table above: the least realizers of 1cβ and of its odd convergents agree in qn + qn+1 − 1 low bits.

    Occupation. Residue occupation is word structure again: m ≡ 5 (mod 8) exactly when d ≥ 3, so words over {1,2} never visit that class. Actual record holders visit it at about 0.18 (delay records) or 0.14 (L₁ records), below the generic 1/4, because they are selected for slow decay.

    Residue tower. At walk length 4 000 the periodic and Sturmian walks are low-discrepancy on every sampled level: their new-sector energy is typically 10−3–10−1 of sampling noise. The two classes separate in the limit N → ∞:

    • Periodic words keep a persistent new-sector energy at a few specific levels, computed exactly in the table, and it is zero at every other level in range. Divisibility of the EOC denominator 3P − 2Q by pe is one way it arises. 13 | 35 − 28 makes w(5/8)'s walk exactly periodic mod 13 (energy 7/5), and 11 | 353 − 284 does the same for w(53/84). It is not the only way: w(12/19) carries energy at 5² and 13², although neither 5 nor 13 divides 312 − 219 = 7153.
    • Sturmian words should have no persistent energy at any level. Argument sketch: the walk's discrete log is A·⌊jλ + ρ⌋ − B·j mod |Ke|, where 2 = gA and 3 = gB. Because λ is irrational, (jλ/n mod 1, j mod n) is jointly equidistributed, which forces every nontrivial character average to 0. The convergence can be slow: the critical words show excess ≈ 24 at 7³ for N = 4 000, but the energy itself falls from 1.5 to 0.065 between N = 4 000 and 32 000. The cause of that slow level was not identified.

    Actual orbits below 106 are at most 195 steps long, so only levels with |Ke| ≤ N/4 are resolvable (e = 1, and e = 2 for p = 5, 7). There their excess (median 0.3–1.2) is indistinguishable from the random control (0.55–1.0). At these resolutions the residue tower separates periodic from Sturmian words, but it does not separate actual orbits from generic words.
